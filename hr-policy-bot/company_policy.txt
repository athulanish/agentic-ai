MERIDIAN DYNAMICS LTD. — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Effective Date: 1 January 2026 | Version 4.2 | Applies to all full-time and part-time employees globally unless a regional addendum overrides a specific clause.

================================================================
SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE
================================================================

This handbook sets out the policies, procedures, and standards of conduct that govern employment at Meridian Dynamics Ltd. ("the Company"). It applies to all employees, contractors engaged for a continuous period exceeding 6 months, and interns, except where a clause explicitly states otherwise. This handbook supersedes all previous versions and verbal agreements. Where local law in a given jurisdiction provides greater employee protection than this handbook, local law takes precedence.

Employees are required to read this handbook in full within their first 14 days of employment and confirm receipt electronically via the HR portal. Managers are responsible for ensuring new hires on their team complete this acknowledgment; failure to do so is flagged in the manager's quarterly compliance review.

This handbook is reviewed annually by the HR Policy Committee, with minor amendments possible at any point during the year following a 30-day notice period published on the internal HR portal.

================================================================
SECTION 2: EMPLOYMENT CLASSIFICATION
================================================================

2.1 Full-Time Employees
Employees working 35 hours or more per week are classified as full-time and are eligible for the full benefits package described in Section 6.

2.2 Part-Time Employees
Employees working between 16 and 34 hours per week are classified as part-time. Part-time employees receive benefits on a pro-rated basis calculated against their contracted hours relative to a 35-hour week.

2.3 Contractors
Contractors are engaged under separate service agreements and are not eligible for the benefits described in this handbook unless explicitly stated in their individual contract. Contractors engaged continuously for more than 6 months must be reviewed by HR for potential reclassification under local employment law.

2.4 Interns
Interns are engaged for a fixed term not exceeding 6 months, renewable once. Interns receive a stipend as set out in their offer letter and are eligible for the Employee Assistance Program described in Section 9.4 but not for annual leave accrual beyond 1 day per completed month.

================================================================
SECTION 3: PROBATION POLICY
================================================================

All new full-time and part-time employees serve a probationary period of 90 calendar days from their start date. During probation, either the employee or the Company may terminate employment with 1 week's written notice, without cause.

Probation may be extended once, for a maximum additional period of 30 days, at the sole discretion of the hiring manager, provided written justification is submitted to HR no later than 5 working days before the original probation end date. An employee cannot be placed on a second extension.

A probation review meeting must occur at the 45-day mark and again at the 80-day mark. Failure to hold the 45-day review does not extend probation automatically but must be documented as a process exception by HR.

Upon successful completion of probation, the employee is confirmed in writing within 5 working days and becomes eligible for the full benefits package, including the pension contribution scheme described in Section 6.3.

================================================================
SECTION 4: WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
================================================================

4.1 Standard Hours
Standard working hours are 35 hours per week, typically structured as 9am to 5pm, Monday to Friday, with a 1-hour unpaid lunch break. Specific team schedules may vary and are set by department heads.

4.2 Core Hours
All employees, regardless of flexible or remote arrangements, must be reachable during core hours of 10am to 4pm in their local time zone, except during approved leave.

4.3 Flexible Start Times
Employees may start between 7:30am and 10am, provided they complete their full contracted hours and attend any meeting scheduled within core hours.

4.4 Overtime
Non-exempt employees (those below Band 5, see Section 12) who work beyond 40 hours in a week are entitled to overtime pay at 1.5 times their standard hourly rate. Overtime must be pre-approved by a manager except in genuine emergencies, which must be logged retroactively within 48 hours. Exempt employees (Band 5 and above) are not eligible for overtime pay but may request time off in lieu at the manager's discretion.

4.5 Attendance and Lateness
Three instances of unapproved lateness exceeding 15 minutes within a rolling 30-day period triggers an automatic conversation with the employee's manager, documented in the HR system but not treated as formal disciplinary action unless it becomes a recurring pattern beyond 90 days.

================================================================
SECTION 5: LEAVE POLICY
================================================================

5.1 Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 21 paid annual leave days per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.75 days per month. Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rated basis. Leave requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal and require manager approval.

5.2 Carryover
Unused annual leave can be carried over into the following calendar year up to a maximum of 5 days. Any leave beyond this cap is forfeited on 31 December unless the employee can demonstrate the leave was denied by the Company for genuine business reasons, in which case HR may approve an exception.

5.3 Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year, separate from annual leave. A medical certificate is required for any absence exceeding 2 consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry over and does not convert to a cash payout upon resignation or termination.

5.4 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave at full salary, followed by an optional 8 additional weeks at 50% salary. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid leave at full salary. Parental leave must be requested at least 8 weeks before the expected start date where reasonably possible.

5.5 Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling) and 2 paid days for the death of an extended family member.

5.6 Jury Duty and Civic Leave
Employees called for jury duty or similar civic obligations receive paid leave for the duration of the obligation, capped at 10 working days per calendar year, beyond which leave becomes unpaid unless otherwise required by local law.

5.7 Unpaid Leave
Employees with at least 1 year of continuous service may request up to 30 days of unpaid leave per calendar year, subject to manager and HR approval, for personal reasons not covered by the categories above.

================================================================
SECTION 6: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
================================================================

6.1 Salary Reviews
Salary reviews occur annually in the Company's fourth fiscal quarter. Reviews consider individual performance ratings, market benchmarking, and overall company performance. There is no guaranteed minimum increase; increases are discretionary.

6.2 Bonus Structure
Eligible employees (Band 2 and above, see Section 12) participate in an annual bonus scheme targeting 10% of base salary at Band 2-3, rising to 20% at Band 4, and 30% at Band 5 and above, subject to individual and company performance against targets set at the start of the fiscal year.

6.3 Pension Contribution
Upon confirmation after probation, the Company matches employee pension contributions up to 6% of base salary, provided the employee contributes at least 3% themselves. Contributions above this matched threshold are not matched further.

6.4 Health Insurance
Full-time employees receive Company-paid private health insurance covering the employee and up to 2 dependents, effective from the first day of the month following their start date. Part-time employees working 24 hours or more per week receive 50% of the premium covered by the Company.

6.5 Stock Options
Employees at Band 3 and above are eligible for a stock option grant upon confirmation after probation, vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff, meaning no options vest until the employee has completed 12 months of continuous service from the grant date.

6.6 Life Insurance
All full-time employees receive Company-paid life insurance coverage equal to 2 times their annual base salary, automatically enrolled with no action required.

================================================================
SECTION 7: EXPENSE POLICY
================================================================

7.1 General Expense Approval
Business expenses under $150 can be self-approved and submitted directly for reimbursement within 30 days of the expense date. Expenses between $150 and $1,000 require manager approval before the expense is incurred. Any expense above $1,000 requires both manager approval and sign-off from the Finance Director.

7.2 Travel Booking
All travel must be booked through the Company's preferred booking platform, Meridian Travel Hub. Bookings made outside this platform are reimbursed at only 70% of the actual cost, except where the platform does not offer a viable route or option, which must be documented and approved by a manager in advance.

7.3 Meal Allowance
Meals during business travel are capped at $60 per day, itemized by receipt. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances, including client entertainment, which is instead processed under the separate Client Entertainment budget code requiring Finance Director approval regardless of amount.

7.4 Accommodation
Hotel accommodation during business travel is capped at $250 per night in standard cities and $400 per night in designated high-cost cities (a list is maintained on the HR portal and reviewed quarterly).

7.5 Mileage
Employees using a personal vehicle for business purposes are reimbursed at $0.65 per mile, calculated from the employee's primary office location or home, whichever is shorter.

7.6 Expense Report Deadlines
Expense reports not submitted within 60 days of the expense date will not be reimbursed except in extraordinary circumstances approved in writing by the Finance Director.

================================================================
SECTION 8: REMOTE AND HYBRID WORK POLICY
================================================================

8.1 Standard Hybrid Arrangement
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with prior manager approval, which does not need to be renewed weekly once an ongoing pattern is agreed and logged in the HR portal.

8.2 Fully Remote Arrangements
Fully remote arrangements (working remotely more than 2 days per week on an ongoing basis) require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed every 6 months to confirm continued business suitability.

8.3 Equipment and Setup
New hires working in a hybrid or remote capacity receive a one-time home office allowance of $300 to cover desk, chair, or monitor costs. The Company does not reimburse ongoing utility costs such as electricity or internet.

8.4 Core Hours While Remote
Employees working remotely must remain reachable during core hours (10am-4pm local time, per Section 4.2) and must attend any meeting flagged "in-person required" in the calendar invite, regardless of their remote work schedule, with reasonable travel time and cost covered per Section 7.

8.5 International Remote Work
Employees wishing to work remotely from a country other than their contracted country of employment for longer than 20 consecutive working days must obtain approval from HR and Legal at least 4 weeks in advance, due to tax and employment law implications.

================================================================
SECTION 9: WORKPLACE CONDUCT AND CULTURE
================================================================

9.1 Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to act with integrity, honesty, and respect toward colleagues, clients, and partners at all times. Any employee found to have deliberately falsified Company records, including expense reports or timesheets, is subject to immediate termination.

9.2 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest to their manager and HR within 10 working days of becoming aware of it. This includes outside employment, financial interests in competitors or suppliers, or personal relationships with direct reports.

9.3 Anti-Harassment Policy
Harassment of any kind, including conduct occurring outside standard working hours at Company-sponsored events, is strictly prohibited and is grounds for immediate termination. Incidents must be reported to HR within 48 hours through the confidential reporting line, which is available 24/7 and can be accessed anonymously.

9.4 Employee Assistance Program
All employees, including interns, have access to the confidential Employee Assistance Program, offering up to 6 free counseling sessions per calendar year covering mental health, financial guidance, and legal consultation.

9.5 Dress Code
The Company maintains a business-casual dress code in all offices. Client-facing roles are expected to dress formally on days with scheduled external client meetings. Remote employees have no mandated dress code except for client-facing video calls.

9.6 Social Media Policy
Employees may identify themselves as working at the Company on personal social media but must include a disclaimer that views expressed are their own. Employees are prohibited from sharing confidential Company information, unreleased product details, or disparaging the Company, colleagues, or clients publicly.

9.7 Use of Company Equipment
Use of Company equipment, including laptops and phones, for personal business ventures is prohibited without written approval from a director. Reasonable personal use (checking personal email, occasional browsing) is permitted.

================================================================
SECTION 10: HEALTH, SAFETY, AND SECURITY
================================================================

10.1 Workplace Safety
All employees must complete mandatory health and safety training within their first 30 days and annually thereafter. Any workplace injury, regardless of severity, must be reported to a manager and logged with HR within 24 hours.

10.2 IT and Data Security
Employees must use multi-factor authentication on all Company systems and are prohibited from storing Company data on personal, unapproved devices or cloud storage services. Any suspected data breach must be reported to the IT Security team immediately, within 1 hour of discovery where possible, and no later than 24 hours under any circumstances.

10.3 Confidentiality
Employees are bound by confidentiality obligations covering Company trade secrets, client data, and unreleased product information, both during employment and for a period of 2 years following termination of employment, regardless of the reason for departure.

10.4 Visitor and Office Access
Visitors must be signed in at reception and accompanied by an employee at all times while on Company premises. Access badges are deactivated automatically on an employee's last working day.

================================================================
SECTION 11: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
================================================================

11.1 Performance Review Cycle
Formal performance reviews occur twice yearly, in June and December. Reviews are based on a combination of manager assessment, peer feedback (minimum 3 peers), and self-assessment.

11.2 Performance Improvement Plans
An employee rated "below expectations" in a formal review may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasting 60 days, with clearly defined, measurable goals agreed jointly by the employee and manager, and reviewed at the 30-day midpoint.

11.3 Outcomes of a PIP
At the end of the 60-day period, the employee either returns to standard performance management, has the PIP extended once by a maximum of 30 days with HR approval, or proceeds to termination if goals were not substantially met.

================================================================
SECTION 12: JOB BANDS AND PROGRESSION
================================================================

The Company uses a 6-tier Band structure, Band 1 (entry-level) through Band 6 (executive leadership). Promotion between bands requires a formal promotion case submitted by the manager, reviewed by a cross-functional promotion panel that meets quarterly. Employees must have completed a minimum of 12 months in their current band before being eligible for promotion consideration, except in exceptional, director-approved cases.

================================================================
SECTION 13: TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT
================================================================

13.1 Learning Budget
Each employee receives an annual learning and development budget of $1,500, which can be used for courses, certifications, conferences, or books relevant to their role, subject to manager approval for amounts over $500.

13.2 Internal Mobility
Employees who have been in their current role for at least 18 months may apply for internal transfers without requiring their current manager's pre-approval to apply, though a transition period of up to 4 weeks' notice may be requested by the current manager.

13.3 Mentorship Program
The Company operates a voluntary mentorship program pairing employees with 2+ years of tenure with newer employees. Participation is logged but not mandatory for either party.

================================================================
SECTION 14: DISCIPLINARY AND GRIEVANCE PROCEDURES
================================================================

14.1 Disciplinary Process
Minor policy violations typically result in a verbal warning, followed by a written warning for a repeated or more serious violation, followed by a final written warning, followed by termination. Serious misconduct (as defined in Sections 9.1 and 9.3) bypasses this escalation and may result in immediate termination.

14.2 Warning Validity Period
Written warnings remain active on an employee's record for 12 months, after which they are not considered in future disciplinary decisions unless a pattern of related conduct resumes.

14.3 Grievance Procedure
Employees wishing to raise a formal grievance should submit it in writing to HR. HR must acknowledge receipt within 2 working days and schedule a grievance meeting within 10 working days. A written outcome is provided within 5 working days of the meeting, with a right to appeal within 10 working days of receiving the outcome.

================================================================
SECTION 15: TERMINATION AND OFFBOARDING
================================================================

15.1 Notice Periods
Following successful completion of probation, the standard notice period is 4 weeks for Bands 1-3 and 8 weeks for Bands 4-6, applicable to both voluntary resignation and Company-initiated termination without cause.

15.2 Garden Leave
The Company reserves the right to place an employee on garden leave for all or part of their notice period, during which the employee remains employed and paid but is not required to attend work or access Company systems.

15.3 Final Pay
Final salary payment, including any accrued but unused annual leave (up to the carryover cap described in Section 5.2), is processed within 10 working days of the employee's last working day.

15.4 Exit Interview
All departing employees are offered a voluntary, confidential exit interview with HR, conducted either in person or via a written questionnaire, at the employee's preference.

15.5 Return of Equipment
All Company equipment must be returned within 5 working days of the last working day. Failure to return equipment may result in the replacement cost being deducted from final pay where permitted by local law.

================================================================
SECTION 16: DIVERSITY, EQUITY, AND INCLUSION
================================================================

16.1 Commitment
The Company is committed to maintaining a workplace free from discrimination based on race, gender, age, disability, religion, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic under applicable law.

16.2 Reporting Discrimination
Any employee experiencing or witnessing discrimination should report it through the same confidential channel described in Section 9.3, with the same 48-hour reporting guidance and identical investigation process.

16.3 Reasonable Accommodations
Employees requiring reasonable accommodations due to disability or medical condition should contact HR, who will work with the employee and, where appropriate, a medical professional, to identify suitable adjustments within 15 working days of the request.

================================================================
SECTION 17: GENERAL PROVISIONS
================================================================

17.1 Policy Amendments
This handbook may be amended by the HR Policy Committee at any time, with material changes communicated to all employees at least 30 days before taking effect, except where a change is required immediately for legal compliance.

17.2 Acknowledgment
Continued employment following publication of an updated handbook constitutes acknowledgment and acceptance of its terms.

17.3 Questions
Any questions regarding this handbook should be directed to the HR team via the internal portal or hr@meridiandynamics.example.

END OF HANDBOOK
